# 9. Submit your commit

To submit the commit, add `LGTM` to it by checking `[]LGTM`.

![check_LGTM_box](../img/check_LGTM_box_img.png)

Now the commit is ready to be submitted! Click the `Submit` button.

![submit_btn](../img/submit_btn_img.png)

You just submitted your first commit!

Submitting can also trigger [CI and CD jobs](../ci-cd/ci-cd.md) for any folder your commit changed.
